Questions

Before you pay

Which email should I use?

The one you sign in to the Voix app with — you log in here with the same email and password, so Pro lands on the right account. If you have not made an account yet, open the app and sign up first; it takes a moment and it is free.

How long until Pro is active?

It is switched on by hand after your payment is confirmed, so it is not instant. If Pro has not appeared, reply in the same WhatsApp chat and it will be sorted there.

Does it renew automatically?

No. There is no subscription and nothing is stored to charge you again — each payment is one-off. When the week or month is up, buy again if you want to continue.

Why is the weekly plan so much cheaper?

₹9 is an introductory price meant for trying Pro, not a permanent rate. It may change. The ₹299 monthly plan is the standard price.

What do I lose if I do not pay?

Very little. Ten of the thirteen conversations and thirteen of the fourteen lessons are free forever, along with grammar correction, speaking practice, streaks and progress. Pro removes the daily practice limit and unlocks the four items marked above.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Corrections run on your phone rather than on a server, which is what makes them appear the moment you finish a sentence. You can practise on a plane.
